# Accommodation
Leisureville Sigiriya is a fully air-conditioned holiday bungalow with 3 bedrooms that can accommodate up to 12 guests. The Master Bedroom has a king bed with an en-suite bathroom. Bedroom 2 and Bedroom 3 each have two twin beds with access to a shared bathroom. All bedrooms have Wi-Fi and ceiling fans.

# Common areas
The bungalow has a spacious covered veranda, a living and dining area, and a tropical garden. Guests can relax outdoors with views of the surrounding paddy fields. Free high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the property.

# Kitchen
A fully equipped self-catering kitchen is available for all guests. Guests who prefer not to cook can arrange for a local cook through the on-site caretaker for an additional charge.

# Caretaker and housekeeping
A resident caretaker is on-site during the day to assist with any needs. Daily housekeeping is included; fresh linen and towels are provided.

# Parking
Free on-site parking is available for guests and their drivers.

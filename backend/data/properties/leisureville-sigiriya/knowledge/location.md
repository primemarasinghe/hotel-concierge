# Location and getting here
Leisureville Sigiriya is situated in Sigiriya, North Central Province, Sri Lanka — just minutes from the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Sigiriya Rock Fortress. Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) in Colombo is approximately 170 km away (around 4 hours by road). Dambulla is 20 km south; Kandy is 90 km south-west (about 1.5 hours); Polonnaruwa is 60 km east.

# Getting around
Tuk-tuks are available near the bungalow for quick local travel. The entrance to Sigiriya Rock Fortress is about 2 km away. We can arrange a car and driver for day trips to Dambulla, Polonnaruwa, Kandy and beyond — ask the caretaker or contact the reservations office.

# Nearby attractions
- **Sigiriya Rock Fortress** (2 km) — UNESCO World Heritage Site, one of Sri Lanka's most visited landmarks
- **Pidurangala Rock** (3 km) — sunrise viewpoint above Sigiriya Rock
- **Dambulla Cave Temple** (20 km) — Golden Temple with 150+ Buddha statues
- **Minneriya National Park** (25 km) — famous elephant gathering, up to 300 elephants
- **Kaudulla National Park** (30 km) — elephant safaris and birdwatching
- **Hiriwadunna Village** (5 km) — authentic rural lake village experience
- **Polonnaruwa Ancient City** (60 km) — best-preserved ancient royal city in Sri Lanka

# Weather
Sigiriya has a warm tropical climate, ranging from 25–35 °C year-round. The best time to visit is from January to April during the dry season. The inter-monsoonal rains (October–November) bring occasional showers but the weather remains warm. Even in the wetter months, Sigiriya sees far less rainfall than the south-west coast.

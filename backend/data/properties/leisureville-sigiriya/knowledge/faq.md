# Check-in and check-out
Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out may be arranged on request; please contact the reservations office at least 24 hours before arrival. Luggage can be stored at the bungalow before check-in and after check-out.

# Reservations and booking
Reservations are handled by the SLT-MOBITEL Leisureville office. You can reach them by phone at 0112021340, available 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM on weekdays (Monday to Friday). You can also visit www.sltmobitel.lk for more information. This concierge can assist with questions, but all bookings are confirmed through the reservations office.

# Pricing
Rooms are priced at LKR 10,000 per room per night. The bungalow has 3 bedrooms, so booking all three rooms costs LKR 30,000 per night for exclusive use by up to 12 guests. Taxes and service charges apply.

# Payment
Payment details will be provided by the SLT-MOBITEL Leisureville reservations office when confirming your booking. Contact them at 0112021340 (weekdays, 9 AM – 5 PM).

# Cancellation policy
Free cancellation is available up to 7 days before arrival. Cancellations made within 7 days of arrival, or no-shows, are charged one night per booked room. During peak season (December 20 – January 7), cancellations within 14 days are non-refundable.

# Maximum guests
The bungalow accommodates up to 12 guests across the 3 bedrooms: Master Bedroom (up to 4), Bedroom 2 (up to 4), and Bedroom 3 (up to 4). Extra guests cannot be accommodated.

# Wi-Fi and connectivity
Free high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the bungalow. Sri Lanka's mobile network (SLT-MOBITEL) has good coverage in the Sigiriya area.

# Pets
Pets are not permitted at the bungalow. Please contact the reservations office if you have any special requirements.

# Children
Children of all ages are welcome. Children under 5 can share existing bedding at no extra charge. Please inform us of the ages of all children when booking.

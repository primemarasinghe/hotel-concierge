# Dining
The Verandah restaurant serves breakfast 7:00–10:30 am, lunch 12:00–3:00 pm and dinner 7:00–10:00 pm. Sri Lankan rice and curry, Western dishes and vegetarian and vegan options are always available. Tell us about allergies when booking. Room service runs 7:00 am–10:30 pm.

# Pool and spa
Infinity pool with lake views, open 7:00 am–8:00 pm, towels provided. The Ayurveda spa offers massages and herbal treatments from 9:00 am to 7:00 pm; booking a day ahead is recommended.

# Other services
Daily housekeeping, laundry service (same day if given before 10 am), currency exchange at reception, a small library, and a tour desk that arranges drivers, guides and train tickets.

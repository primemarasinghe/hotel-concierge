# Check-in and check-out
Check-in is from 2:00 pm and check-out is by 11:00 am. Early check-in and late check-out are free when the room is available on the day; to guarantee them, book the previous or following night. Luggage can be stored at reception before check-in and after check-out.

# Children and extra beds
Children of all ages are welcome. Children under 6 stay free using existing bedding. A baby cot is free on request. An extra bed for a child (6–11) costs USD 20 per night and is available in Deluxe Garden and Superior Lake View rooms only. The Heritage Suite is adults only.

# Payment
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and cash in LKR or USD. Online bookings are confirmed by our reservations team by email with a secure payment link. We never ask for card details over chat or WhatsApp.

# Cancellation policy
Free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival. Cancellations within 7 days, or no-shows, are charged one night. During the Festive peak and the Esala Perahera festival, the full stay is non-refundable within 14 days of arrival.

# Wi-Fi, parking and smoking
Free high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the hotel. Free on-site parking for guests, including a space for a driver; driver accommodation is available at USD 15 per night including meals. All rooms are non-smoking; smoking is permitted in the garden pavilion.

# Pets
Sorry, we cannot accommodate pets.

# Accessibility
Two Deluxe Garden Rooms are on the ground floor with step-free access and grab rails. The lake-view rooms and suites are on upper floors without a lift.

# Temple dress code
When visiting the Temple of the Tooth, shoulders and knees must be covered and shoes removed. We lend sarongs at reception.

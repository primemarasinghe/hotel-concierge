# Location and getting here
The hotel is on Anniewatte hill, about 1.5 km (5 minutes by car) from Kandy Lake and the Temple of the Tooth. Kandy Railway Station is about 2.5 km away. Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) is roughly 3 hours by road.

# Getting around
Tuk-tuks wait near the hotel gate; a ride to the lake costs about LKR 400–600. We can arrange a car with an English-speaking driver for day trips, priced by itinerary.

# Weather
Kandy is mild year-round, around 20–29 °C. Showers are common in the afternoons from October to December. Evenings can feel cool, so bring a light layer.
